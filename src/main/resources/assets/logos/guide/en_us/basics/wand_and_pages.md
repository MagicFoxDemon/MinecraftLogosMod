To cast a spell you need two tools: a **wand**, which determines channeling speed and how many spells you can carry at once, and a **spell page**, which holds the spell itself and caps how much mana a single cast may inject.

## Wood Wands
Any of the seven vanilla wood types crafts a wood wand with the same recipe. Each wood wand holds 3 spell pages.
{{recipe logos:oak_wand}}
{{items logos:oak_wand logos:spruce_wand logos:birch_wand logos:jungle_wand logos:acacia_wand logos:dark_oak_wand logos:cherry_wand}}

Higher-tier wands offer more spell slots and faster channeling, and holding or carrying one increases your mana cap and regeneration. Craft advanced tiers with alchemical materials or purchase them from Warlock villagers.

## Spell Pages
A piece of paper and a gold nugget craft a [Basic Spell Page](item:logos:spell_page). Blank pages must be inscribed with a spell before they can be cast.
{{recipe logos:spell_page}}

Pages come in seven grades from lowest to highest: Basic, Crystalline, Echoing, Transmuted, Chalk, Firmament, and Sage's. Higher grades hold more mana per cast, letting larger spells run to completion. Rewriting a spell never changes the page's grade.

## Inscription Table
The workbench used to compose and write spells, crafted from a Basic Spell Page, a book and quill, and a lectern.
{{recipe logos:inscription_table}}

Once your tools are ready, head to the Inscription Table to write [your first spell](first_spell).
