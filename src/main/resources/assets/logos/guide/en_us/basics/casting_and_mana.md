## Charging and Casting
Hold **right-click** with a wand carrying a spell page to channel mana into the spell; once it finishes charging, **release right-click** to cast. You can also keep holding after reaching full charge and release when the moment is right.

If you release early, switch held items, or die mid-charge, any mana already channeled drifts away as free mana rather than returning to your pool.

A wand can hold multiple pages at once. Press **R** to open the Spell Wheel and choose which spell is active.

A wand in your off hand can cast too: whenever your main-hand item has no right-click action, right-clicking delegates to the off-hand wand.

## Staffs
Staffs reverse the rhythm of wands: **hold left-click** beforehand to charge mana into the staff's internal reserve, then **right-click** in battle to cast instantly without waiting to charge. Upgrade a wood wand with logs and gold nuggets to craft a wood staff.

## Passive Spells
Besides casting at will, a spell node's trigger can be set to **When hurt**, **When attacking**, or **On a kill**. Passive spells fire automatically whenever the condition occurs as long as the wand or codex is carried in hand.

## Spell Codex
A [Spell Codex](item:logos:spell_codex) stores multiple spell pages inside one book. Held in your off hand, it lets any wand in your main hand cast every spell inside the codex directly.

## Mana and Growth
Casting draws from your personal mana pool, shown in the corner HUD while holding a wand, which regenerates naturally over time. You can begin channeling only when your current mana covers the spell's full injection cost.

Your max mana cap grows through four forms of mastery:
- **Cultivation**: Increases with total lifetime experience earned; spending experience or dying never lowers it.
- **Vigor**: Bonus max health above 20 points adds to your mana cap.
- **Armor and Toughness**: Higher equipped armor and armor toughness raise your cap.
- **Practice**: Every point of mana spent casting spells steadily expands your reserve.

Carrying higher-tier wands or staffs also raises your mana cap and regeneration. Run `/logos mana` to inspect a full breakdown of your current cap.
