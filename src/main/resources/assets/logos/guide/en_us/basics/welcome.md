Logos has no preset "Fireball" or "Chain Lightning" spells. Here, magic is a language: you compose spells stroke by stroke from **runes**, inscribe them onto **spell pages**, and hang them on a **wand** to cast.

The very same runes become completely different spells when wired another way. A fireball is simply "a projectile that deals damage on hit, then scorches enemies nearby when it expires"; chain lightning is "a ray that hits an enemy, then refracts from that target toward the next foe." Whatever you can write, you can cast.

## Quick Start
1. Craft an [oak wand and a few spell pages](wand_and_pages).
2. Inscribe [your first spell](first_spell) at an Inscription Table.
3. Hang the page on your wand to [charge and cast](casting_and_mana).
