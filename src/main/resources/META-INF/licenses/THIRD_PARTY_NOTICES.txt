Logos bundles the following mods, unmodified, as nested jars (NeoForge Jar-in-Jar) under META-INF/jarjar/.
Each keeps its own license, reproduced in this folder. They are not covered by the license of Logos.
NeoForge loads the highest version present, so a newer copy installed separately replaces the bundled one.
